JobHunterX · free & open source Windows · macOS · Linux

Jobs that fit you.Measured, not guessed.

Made first for early-career job seekers in India — Hyderabad, Bengaluru and remote roles — who are tired of being applicant number fifteen hundred.

JobHunterX reads your resume, finds real openings on employers’ own job boards, and shows you why each one fits — and how likely a person is to actually read your application. Then it writes honest documents and helps you apply, while you watch.

Piece 00 — the problem with more

Applying to more jobs stopped working.

A public post on the big job sites can collect hundreds of applicants within days. Recruiters read the first few dozen and filter on years, current company and notice period. With one or two years of experience, you are often filtered out before anyone reads a word.

i

Noisy lists

Big job boards mix in roles from other countries, other careers and other seniority levels. You scroll past them yourself, one by one.

ii

Late arrivals

By the time a popular listing reaches you, the crowd is already there. The same role is often posted first on the company’s own careers page.

iii

Silent scores

Most tools say “92% match” and stop. They don’t say what fits, what’s missing, or why a job with all your keywords still won’t take you.

iv

Inflated resumes

Rewriting tools happily add skills you don’t have. That gets caught in the interview — the worst place to be caught.

“Apply to more” doesn’t work.
Being seen early, on the right channel, does.— the idea JobHunterX is built on

Piece 01 — the fitting, in seven parts

How JobHunterX works, one piece at a time.

Pick any part, or press play and watch the whole fitting. Each scene is a small illustration of the real workflow — the data in them is an example, and nothing here talks to a server.

Part a · the starting point

Upload your resume once. Then check what it understood.

JobHunterX reads your PDF in the background and builds a profile: your roles, your skills and where you used them, your location. Years of experience are calculated from your dates — total, without internships, and internships alone — so the numbers are never guessed.

A screen called “how we see you” shows the result. Fix anything that’s wrong before a single job is searched.

Part b · understanding your goals

Tell it what you actually want.

Add as many job titles and cities as you like, choose remote, hybrid or on-site, and give your current and expected pay and your notice period. These become the measuring rules for every job it finds.

Indian salaries are understood the way people write them — “12–18 LPA”, “₹12L”, “1.2 Cr”. Type 14 and it means 14 LPA.

Part c · discovering real openings

It goes to the source: the company’s own job board.

An employer job board is the careers page a company runs itself, usually through a hiring system such as Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby. Posts there are first-hand, and often appear before the big public sites copy them.

Web search results are treated as leads only. Each posting is then checked: is it real, reachable, still open — and in your country? Duplicates are merged and the employer’s copy wins. Unknown stays unknown.

While the app is open, it re-checks your watchlist companies’ boards every 4 hours. New roles land in a “New” tab.

Part d · intelligent matching

Two measurements, kept apart.

Fit answers “am I right for this job?”. Hard checks come first — career track, years, location, must-have skills, notice period. A failed hard check caps the score, so matching keywords can never lift a job you can’t get.

Chance to be seen answers “will a person read my application?” — from how fresh the post is, where it’s posted, and how crowded it looks.

Pick a role. Candidate: about 1.5 years of professional AI work in Hyderabad, not relocating. These cases mirror an example in the project’s test suite; the numbers are illustrative.

Part e · honest application documents

Three different documents. None of them invented.

For a job you pick, it writes a one-page resume, a cover letter under 300 words, and a CV of your whole career. Five ATS-safe styles; download any as PDF.

Every AI-written line is fact-checked against your profile. A new number, tool, employer or claim that isn’t in your profile is rejected, and your own words stay. Skills the job wants that you don’t have are listed for you — never added.

Click a paper to bring it forward. Press “Run the fact-check” to see one edit kept and one refused.
Illustration · example edits
Part f · the browser agent

It fills the form. You can grab the wheel any time.

Press Auto-apply and JobHunterX checks your documents, writes any that are missing, then opens a private browser streamed inside the app. Every step is listed in plain words.

Stop now stops at once. Take over pauses the agent and sends your clicks to the page — for a CAPTCHA, a login or anything you want to do yourself. Give back and it carries on from where you left it.

    Part g · tracking progress

    Every application hangs where you can see it.

    The tracker follows the journey in six stages — Saved, Preparing, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Closed — with one click to move a job to its next stage, and a hint for what to do there.

    You can log how you applied — the company’s site, a referral, a note to the team — and what happened. Over time the Results view shows which routes actually get you interviews.

    Press “Next stage” to move the example tag. A tracker shows progress — it can’t promise an offer.

    Piece 02 — the pattern book

    Everything on the table, and what each tool is for.

    Two ideas set JobHunterX apart. The rest is careful workmanship around them.

    Signature idea · one

    Chance to be seen.

    Shown next to every fit score and never mixed into it. Each signal is listed with its reason, so you can decide where your evening is best spent.

    SignalRaises the chanceLowers the chance
    FreshnessPosted in the last 1–3 daysOlder than 2–4 weeks
    ChannelThe company’s own careers board; curated boardsA public LinkedIn / Naukri / Indeed listing
    ApplicantsThe page shows a small countThe page shows 50+ or 200+
    Direct routeThe post gives an email for your resume—
    Notice—“Immediate joiners” while yours is longer

    Sort by Best chance (fit and reach together), Best fit, or Least crowded.

    Signature idea · two

    A researched watchlist.

    About 130 companies in Hyderabad, Bengaluru and remote India — AI startups, product companies and global capability centres. For each: what real AI work happens there, whether it hires people with 1–3 years, ratings, red flags, and how crowded applying is.

    Their own job boards are checked every 4 hours while the app is open, so you can apply among the first.

    The full kit.

    Each line is a real part of the app, described the way the project’s own README describes it.

    1. UnderstandReads you properly

      Career tracks, realistic titles, and skills marked by evidence — used at work, used in projects, or only listed. Invented skills are dropped.

    2. PreferencesMany titles, many cities

      Remote, hybrid or relocation; current and expected CTC; notice period. Several people can share one install, each with their own profile.

    3. DiscoverEmployer boards first

      Reads public job APIs of hiring systems like Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and more. Also YC startups, the r/developersIndia hiring thread and founders’ “we’re hiring” posts.

    4. WatchlistCompanies worth watching

      Researched company cards with verdicts, ratings and red flags. “Check for new roles now” shows the check live, step by step.

    5. ExplainWhy this score

      A 0–100 score and a verdict — strong, good, stretch, weak, incompatible — with strengths, gaps, unknowns and the exact reason a job was rejected.

    6. ReachSeen early, not buried

      Freshness, channel, applicant counts and direct routes, each with its points. The Connector drafts a short note to a real person — you choose who.

    7. GenerateResume, CV, cover letter

      For a matched job, or for any job you paste in. One or two columns, five ATS-safe styles, PDF in the style you see.

    8. Edit honestlyAn editor that can’t lie

      “Edit with AI” proposes small named edits. Each is fact-checked; contact details, dates, companies and degrees can’t be touched. Nothing saves until you say so.

    9. ApplyA browser agent you can watch

      Streams into the app with plain-language steps. Handles hidden checkboxes, college vs university, and phone numbers without a double +91.

    10. Take overYour hand on it

      Stop at once, take over, give back, continue after a restart, or mark “I submitted it” yourself. Progress is saved, so nothing starts over.

    11. TrackThe whole journey in one bar

      Saved → Preparing → Applied → Interviewing → Offer, with Results that show interviews per 10 applications by route.

    12. ProtectA scam filter

      Fees, UPI payments, WhatsApp-only contact, OTP requests and weeks-old websites are flagged. Critical hits are never recommended.

    13. SecureCareful by default

      Same-origin requests, a CSRF guard, SSRF-safe fetching of job pages, upload limits, and a zero-spend guard that blocks paid search calls.

    Made for 0–2 years

    Reachable jobs, a safety net, honest results.

    Experience bands, not a hard cap

    Within reach · apply directly or with a referral · stretch · out of range. A “1–3 years” post is read as a preference, the way a person reads it.

    A safety-net tab

    Large IT-services fresher programmes get their own tab instead of crowding your main list.

    Immediate-joiner aware

    With no notice period, “immediate joiner” posts rank up. With a long one, they’re flagged.

    Honest forms

    The browser agent never rounds your experience up on knock-out questions.

      Piece 04 — cut honestly

      What it won’t do.

      JobHunterX isn’t a mass-application bot. It measures, explains and helps — and some lines it simply doesn’t cross.

      What it will do
      • Explain every score, including what’s unknown
      • Show real progress — counts the server reports, never estimated from time
      • Show every agent step, and stop the moment you press Stop
      • Keep your data in your own app folder

      • Invent a skill, a number or an employer.

        Generated text is checked against your profile. Anything new is rejected and your own words are kept.

      • Round your experience up.

        Years are computed from your dates, and the agent answers knock-out questions with the true number.

      • Spray your resume across hundreds of jobs.

        Auto-apply runs for the job you choose. The point is fewer, better-fitted applications.

      • Guess an email address or scrape people.

        The Connector uses only an email the posting itself gives, and search links where you choose the person.

      • Hide why a job was rejected.

        “Requires 6+ years; you have ~1.6.” The reason is written down, every time.

      Piece 05 — take it home

      Install it like any other app.

      Double-click, and it opens in its own window. Everything runs on your computer, and new versions offer to install themselves.

      Windows installer

      For Windows 10 & 11

      • 64-bit (x64)
      • No admin rights needed
      Download for Windows.exe installer · newest release
      • Windows .msi package JobHunterX_windows_x64.msi — for managed installs
      Install in three steps
      1. Run JobHunterX_windows_x64-setup.exe.
      2. Follow the installer. It installs for your user only.
      3. Open JobHunterX from the Start menu. The first screen asks for a free AI key — or press Start free.
      About the blue warningThe app isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. If you downloaded the file from the official kvcops/JobHunterX release, choose More info → Run anyway. If it came from anywhere else, don’t.
      macOS disk image

      For Apple Silicon Macs

      • macOS 11 or later
      • M1, M2, M3… (arm64)
      Download for macOS.dmg · newest release
      • Intel Mac? Run from source No Intel build is published. The source version runs on macOS too.
      Install in three steps
      1. Open JobHunterX_darwin_aarch64.dmg.

      3. First launch only: right-click → Open, then confirm.

      Piece 06 — the pattern itself

      Run it from source, in one command.

      Never used a terminal? That’s fine. You’ll copy two short lines, paste them, and press Enter. A script does the rest.

      Your computer
      What you need
      • Internet for the first setup.
      • Git to copy the project — or download the ZIP instead.
      • Google Chrome, used by Auto-apply.
      • One free AI key — or none, with “Start free”.

      You don’t need to install Python. The script fetches Python 3.11 through uv if it’s missing.

      Repository
      The project’s source code, kept on GitHub.
      Clone
      Make a copy of that code on your computer.
      Terminal
      A window where you type commands. On Windows it’s called PowerShell.
      AI API key
      A password-like code that lets the app use an AI service. Keep it private.

      1. Open a terminal

        Press the Start button, type PowerShell, and open it.

        Press ⌘ + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter.

        Open your terminal app — often Ctrl + Alt + T.

      2. Copy the project and step inside it

        This downloads the code into a folder called jobhunterx, then moves into it.

        PowerShell
        PS> git clone https://github.com/kvcops/jobhunterx.git
PS> cd jobhunterx

      3. Start it

        PowerShell
        PS> powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File start.ps1

        Prefer clicking? Open the jobhunterx folder and double-click start.bat. The -ExecutionPolicy Bypass part only applies to this one script run.

        Terminal
        $ bash start.sh

      4. Let the first run set things up

        The first time, the script installs uv (a fast Python installer), makes a private virtual environment, installs the packages — around 30 seconds — creates the settings file, checks everything loads, and opens the app in your browser once it answers.

      5. Add a free AI key — or don’t

        The first screen asks for one key, shows where to get it for free, tests it with a single call and saves it. Google AI Studio is the easiest. NVIDIA, Groq and Mistral keys work too; two or three make searches faster, because each free plan allows only a few calls a minute.

        No key at all? Press Start free to use Kilo’s free models. Note: those models are marked as possibly training on your prompts, and your resume text is part of those prompts.

      6. Use it, stop it, start it again

        The app lives at http://127.0.0.1:9000 — an address on your own computer. To stop it, press Ctrl + C in the terminal. Next time, run the same start command; it skips setup and just starts.

      Prefer doing it by hand? The advanced path

      1 · Install uv

      PS> powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://astral.sh/uv/install.ps1 | iex"
      $ curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh

      Close the terminal, open a new one, and check with uv --version. Can’t run the installer? pip install uv works too.

      2 · Make and turn on a virtual environment

      PS> uv venv --python 3.11
PS> .venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1

      PowerShell says “running scripts is disabled”? Run Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope CurrentUser RemoteSigned once, then try again.

      $ uv venv --python 3.11
$ source .venv/bin/activate

      3 · Install the packages

      PS> uv pip install -r requirements.txt

      4 · Keys (optional — the app can ask for them)

      PS> copy jobhunterx\.env.example jobhunterx\.env
      $ cp jobhunterx/.env.example jobhunterx/.env

      Then paste your key after GOOGLE_API_KEY= in that file. Never share the file or post it in an issue.

      5 · Start

      PS> cd jobhunterx
PS> python -m jobhunterx.api.main

      Open http://127.0.0.1:9000. Next time: turn the venv on, cd jobhunterx, and run the same start line.

      If something goes wrong
      ProblemFix
      Resume upload keeps loadingNo real AI key yet. Restart the app; the first screen asks for one. Placeholder text like your_gemini_api_key doesn’t count.
      uv not foundClose and reopen the terminal after installing it, or use pip install uv.
      No module named jobhunterxRun the start line from inside the inner jobhunterx folder, with the venv on.
      Port 9000 is busySet PORT=9001 in jobhunterx/.env and open http://127.0.0.1:9001.
      Auto-apply can’t open a browserInstall Google Chrome.
      Searches are slowAdd a second free key (Groq, for example). Free plans allow only a few calls a minute each.
      Piece 07 — the care label

      Read before use: privacy, needs, limits.

      Every good garment comes with a label. Here’s what you should know before you start.

      Where your data lives

      • The desktop app keeps your data in its own app folder on your computer.
      • From source, your profile, jobs and documents are in a local SQLite database, and keys in a local .env file that git ignores.
      • There is no JobHunterX account or cloud. In the optional multi-user server mode, users’ saved keys are encrypted.

      What leaves your computer

      • To read your resume and job posts, their text is sent to the AI provider you choose — Google, NVIDIA, Groq, Mistral, or Kilo’s free models.
      • Kilo’s free models are marked as possibly training on your prompts. If that matters to you, add any free key and turn Kilo off in Settings → AI providers.
      • Searching and applying visit job sites and employer boards on the public web, the way a browser would.

      Honest limits

      • Free AI plans allow only a few calls a minute, so a search with one key can be slow. A second key helps.
      • CAPTCHAs, logins and one-time codes may need you — that’s what Take over is for.
      • The installers aren’t code-signed or notarized yet. macOS builds are Apple Silicon only; Linux builds are x86-64.
      • No tool can promise interviews. JobHunterX helps you spend effort where it’s most likely to be seen.

      Who it’s cut for

      Job seekers in India. Jobs outside your country are dropped before scoring. The researched watchlist covers Hyderabad, Bengaluru and remote India today; the README says more South Indian cities come next. Its sharpest tuning is for people with 0–3 years of experience.

      Piece 08 — questions at the counter

      Asked, answered.

      What exactly is JobHunterX?

      An app that runs on your computer and helps with the whole job search: it understands your resume and goals, finds real openings, explains how well each fits and how likely you are to be seen, writes honest application documents, helps fill applications with a browser agent you can take over, and tracks everything.

      Is it free?

      Yes. The app is open source under the MIT license. It uses AI services through their free tiers — you bring a free key, or use Kilo’s free models with no key. Provider limits and terms can change; check each provider’s page.

      Is the source code available?

      All of it, on GitHub. You can read it, run it from source, fork it and contribute.

      Does it apply to every job automatically?

      No. Searching runs on its own, but Auto-apply starts when you press it on a job you’ve chosen. The agent fills the form while you watch the live view, and you can stop it at once, take over, or finish and mark it submitted yourself.

      Can I review an application first?

      Yes. Your documents are ready to read and edit before Auto-apply opens the browser, and every agent step is shown in plain words. Take over at any point to check fields yourself.

      What happens with a login or CAPTCHA?

      The application shows as needing you. Take over, click and type right in the live view, then press Continue. Progress is saved, so nothing starts over — even after an app restart.

      Do I need an AI API key?

      Not strictly. “Start free” uses Kilo’s free models with no key, though they may train on prompts. A free Google AI Studio key is the easiest private option; NVIDIA, Groq and Mistral keys also work.

      Where is my data stored?

      On your computer — the desktop app’s own folder, or a local database when run from source. Text the AI needs to read is sent to the AI provider you choose.

      Windows, macOS and Linux?

      Installers exist for Windows 10/11 (x64), macOS 11+ on Apple Silicon, and 64-bit Linux (AppImage, .deb, .rpm). Other machines, like Intel Macs, can run it from source.

      How is this different from job alerts?

      Alerts forward matching keywords. JobHunterX checks that a posting is real and open, reads it, applies hard checks like years and location, explains the score, estimates your chance of being seen, and goes to employers’ own boards before the crowds arrive.

      Does it guarantee interviews?

      No — nothing can. It helps you apply to fewer, better-fitted jobs, earlier, with honest documents. The Results view shows what works for you over time.

      Can I run it locally only?

      It always runs locally — on 127.0.0.1 — unless you choose the optional server mode for several people. It still needs the internet to search for jobs and reach AI providers.
